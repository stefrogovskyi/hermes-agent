# Avalanche Agency Brand Book & Design System (v2.2)

Quick reference for generating visual assets, social creatives, Canva templates, and web UI components compliant with `https://aavalanche.com/brand`.

## ⚠️ Critical Rule: Agency vs. Client Separation
* **Avalanche Agency & Enlight Group** (`aavalanche.com`) is the primary agency.
* **Navo** (`navo24.com`) is a distinct client/product in the portfolio.
* **NEVER confuse or mix the two design systems**:
  * Navo uses Ranade Ultralight, Switzer, JetBrains Mono, 7-ring concentric disc logo, and paper/ink styling.
  * Avalanche Agency uses Inter (Bold/Extra-Bold), geometric double "A" monogram, Brand Sky Blue `#5FB3F9`, Electric Blue `#2563EB`, and Dark Slate Navy `#0F172A`.

---

## 1. Logo System & Assets
* **Mark**: Stylized geometric double "A" monogram in a rounded sky-blue square.
* **Lockup**: Logo badge paired with bold Slate Navy typography `Avalanche Agency`.
* **Mobile Badge**: On mobile screens (≤ 768px), display 38px logo badge only (hide text).
* **Asset URL**: `https://aavalanche.com/avalanche_logo.png`

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens
* **Brand Sky Blue**: `#5FB3F9` (Primary identity, badges, borders, highlights)
* **Electric Blue**: `#2563EB` (Primary CTA buttons, active links, gradient anchors)
* **Deep Royal Blue**: `#1E40AF` (Deep accents, button hover states)
* **Dark Slate Navy**: `#0F172A` (Dark ground, text headlines, executive cards)
* **Soft Sky Tint**: `#F0F7FF` (Light ground, card backgrounds, subtle pill tags)
* **Success Emerald**: `#10B981` (Verified case badges, positive metrics)
* **Border Lines**: `#1E293B` (on dark) / `#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1` (on light)

---

## 3. Typography (Inter Hierarchy)
* **Hero Headline (H1)**: Inter 900 Black
* **Section Headline (H2)**: Inter 800 Extra-Bold
* **Card Titles (H3/H4)**: Inter 700 Bold
* **Body & Lead**: Inter 400 Regular / 600 Semi-Bold
* **Badges & Pills**: Inter 700 Bold, uppercase

---

## 4. Four Core Pillars
1. 🌐 **Infrastructure**: Domain selection & purchase, SSL, DNS, email server setup, high-speed hosting.
2. 💻 **Development**: Custom web engineering, SaaS, Monobank acquiring, multi-language portals.
3. ✍️ **Content & AI**: Brand storytelling, copywriting, professional translations, 24/7 AI agents.
