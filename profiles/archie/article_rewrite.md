## Calculating Monthly Financial Leakage

Unfavorable tariffs and unmonitored routes deplete working capital without triggering obvious alerts. Quantifying these ongoing losses requires breaking down the core operational math.

- Routine manual mistakes: 5 logistics specialists × 20 hours per routine task × 30 USD cost per hour = $3,000 monthly losses. This consumes 10-30% of working time.
- Human factor errors in booking and route data: 4% of orders with errors × 200 USD average loss per error × 1000 orders = $80,000 annual losses. This eats 2% to 5% of annual logistics turnover.
- Suboptimal freight procurement: Difference between the best tariff on the market (50 USD) and actual tariff × 10,000 units volume per year = $500,000 annual losses. Overpaying inflates freight and procurement expenses by 10-20%.
- Delivery status tracking chaos: 2 hours spent researching delivery status × 25 USD logistician hourly rate × 500 total cases = $25,000 losses. Total impact includes direct costs and reputational damage.
- Delayed rebooking decisions: 1000 USD cost of lost opportunity due to 1-2 day delay × 20 such situations per year = $20,000 losses. This erodes 5-15% of the transaction margin.

No human dispatch team can track every global tariff shift while locking in transient spot rates.
