# Full Body Text

The SeaRates and Digital Freight Alliance teams recently returned from Dubai Expo City after attending the Global Freight Summit 2024. Centered on the theme 'Acting on the Opportunities of a Changing World', the event gathered over 8,000 daily attendees, bringing together market leaders across global trade and supply chain automation.

## Global Trade Leaders Gather in Dubai

Day 1 opened with greetings from Mike Bhaskaran, COO at DP World, followed by a Q&A session with Jim Yong Kim, 12th President of the World Bank. The initial agenda featured speeches by Tony Fernandes, CEO of Capital A and founder of AirAsia, Hadi Badri, CEO of Dubai Economic Development Corporation, H.E. Sultan Ahmed bin Sulayem, Group Chairman and CEO of DP World, H.E. Dr. Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi, UAE Minister of State for Foreign Trade, and H.S.H. Prince Michael von Liechtenstein, Founder & Chairman of Geopolitical Intelligence Services.

Product presentations and leadership insights rounded out the first day. Ekaterina Komarova, Head of Digital Department at SeaRates, presented unique features of SeaRates IT products designed for freight and global supply chain management. Mariia Slabenko, Head of DFA, presented Premium Membership in the Digital Freight Alliance network, explaining its benefits for freight forwarder digitalization and gathering direct feedback from members. Later in the day, global leadership expert and bestselling author Robin Sharma introduced the concept of mindset mastery for leaders.

## AI, Robotics, and Supply Chain Innovation

Day 2 shifted focus toward technological progress and industry discussions. Panel sessions covered AI and robotics for trade and logistics, global shipping trends, collaborative solutions for sustainable development, and cold chain mastering. Celebrity guests Shah Rukh Khan, Bollywood actor and investor, alongside Ryan Reynolds, actor and investor, engaged directly with attendees throughout the day.

The summit concluded on Day 3 with deep dives into resilient economies, using lessons from Switzerland as a guide. Discussions examined technology deployment across AI predictive analytics, robotics in warehouse automation, blockchain, and digital twins. Sessions also addressed open collaboration platforms, brand building, and trade dynamics across key regional lanes including the Middle East, Latin America, Africa, APAC, Europe, and the Americas. Wikipedia co-founder Jimmy Wales led a key session on open collaboration, sharing how collective intelligence can create transparency and trust in global trade.

## Driving Digitalization Across Shipping Networks

For SeaRates and the Digital Freight Alliance, the summit offered a direct platform to engage with global logistics professionals. SeaRates CEO Stefan Rogovskiy expressed pride in being part of the DP World family, emphasizing three days of valuable networking, business automation, AI engagement, and logistics robotization. He pointed out that SeaRates digital tools and DFA Premium Membership generated strong attention across the market.

Mariia Slabenko highlighted the value of connecting with over 8,000 visitors, sharing how DFA membership supports digital transformation for freight forwarders while gathering actionable feedback from the network. GFS 2024 served as a milestone for global logistics, sparking actionable strategies for adopting AI, blockchain, and robotics across supply chains.
