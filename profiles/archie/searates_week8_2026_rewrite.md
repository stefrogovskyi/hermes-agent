TITLE: SeaRates Week 8, 2026: Parcel Tracking Goes Live, Carrier Network Grows

META-TITLE: SeaRates Week 8, 2026 Update: Parcel Tracking Live

META-DESCRIPTION: Parcel Tracking is live with predictive ETA, 2417 carriers now supported. Container tracking expands to 224 lines, Air Tracking now covers 446 airlines.

BODY:

A tracking number is a small promise, and this week SeaRates kept a few more of them. The team keeps shipping regular updates instead of one big overhaul, prioritizing changes that actually help with daily operations. Last week's roundup is still up if you missed it.

## Container Tracking: 224 lines and counting

Two more shipping lines joined the network this week: Africa Express Line and New Guinea Pacific Line. That brings the Container Tracking API to 224 supported shipping lines in total. Behind the scenes, the team also tightened its data collaboration with a long list of carriers: Avana Logistek, DHL Global Forwarding, SITC Container Lines, Seaboard Marine, Yang Ming, COSCO, Regional Container Lines (RCL), Cosco Specialized, CEVA Logistics, UWL, ECU Worldwide, Sealead Shipping, Interasia Lines, Milaha - Qatar Navigation Q.P.S.C., DP World Logistics, Wan Hai, Transit Fruits Eagle, and National Shipping of America.

## Parcel Tracking is live

The Parcel Tracking web tool has officially launched. You can now track parcels worldwide and see predictive ETA, status, movement, route details, and other shipping insights, updated in real time. It lives inside SeaRates' unified Tracking System, alongside container and air tracking.

Carrier coverage keeps growing too. Collaboration with couriers, including FedEx, has improved, and the Parcel Tracking API now supports 2417 global and regional carriers.

## Air Tracking: one new airline, 446 total

My Freighter (Centrum Air) is now supported, bringing the total to 446 airlines in the Air Tracking API; the full list sits in the API documentation. Support also got better for airlines already on the platform: FedEx Express, Air Canada, Japan Airlines, Air India, Air Serbia, Turkish Airlines, Aloha Air Cargo, Qatar Airways, UPS Air Cargo, and Qantas.

## Schedules, quietly improved

Ship Schedules collaboration improved with Sinokor, Heung-A, Seaboard, Gemadept, and Crowley, all by Points, plus Namsung by Port. On the flight side, Flight Schedules collaboration improved with airlines including Lufthansa. The API documentation for Flight Schedules is on the Developer Portal.

## Rates and search

The Geocoding API's Autocomplete now supports airport search by name and IATA code. The Rate Management System inside your VO and the Freight Index tool also picked up a round of upgrades for calculating, searching, and managing freight rates.

## What's coming

- Load Calculator Web 3.0, with a new design and new features
- A new Map Platform
- Geocoding API integrated with Logistics Explorer
- Extended Inbox integration across Logistics Explorer, Bookings, and Notifications
