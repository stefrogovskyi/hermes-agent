# SeaRates Updates: New Tracking APIs and Load Calculator

Logistics teams require direct access to operational data, clear pricing structures, and consistent carrier coverage across modes. The week 17 platform release introduces transparent tariff options for cargo planning, published technical guidelines for overland integration, expanded terminal connectivity, and broader tracking coverage across ocean and air carriers.

## Load Calculator Pricing and Developer Resources

Selecting freight parameters now comes with immediate financial visibility through our new load calculator pricing page. Shippers and forwarders can evaluate tariffs directly inside the interface, selecting plans that match their monthly booking volumes and safety requirements.

Developers integrating land freight capabilities can access the newly added road tracking API documentation on our Developer Portal. The reference details exact request formats, authentication parameters, and payload structures required to embed overland status updates into external management systems.

## Terminal, Container, and Air Tracking Improvements

Port connectivity expands with the addition of CMA TERMINALS KHALIFA PORT to our Khalifa Port terminal API coverage. Port calls at this Middle Eastern hub can now be monitored alongside existing global facilities.

Container tracking API capabilities expand through support for the PSA BDP shipping line. Data exchange quality and operational sync have also been updated across several ocean carriers, including Hyundai Merchant Marine (HMM), Avana Logistek, Sealead Shipping, Lucky Logistics, and CMA CGM.

Air tracking airlines support now covers expanded status updates across ten major international carriers. Cargo operations using Shenzhen Airlines, Flydubai, Finnair, Air Canada, SF Airlines, United Airlines, Air India, Southwest Airlines, EVA Air, and ITA Airways receive more consistent milestone tracking.

## Ship Schedules and Ongoing Developments

Vessel schedule queries have been refined based on carrier data structures. Schedule lookups now support Seaboard by Points, KOS by Vessel, along with CMA CGM and ONE by Port queries.
