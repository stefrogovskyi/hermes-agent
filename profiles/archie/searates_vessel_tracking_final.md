Open the SeaRates Tools page and pick the Vessel Tracking web tool. That's where global, real-time tracking starts.

### Step 2: Get the voyage overview
Pick a vessel and a card pops up with a quick voyage snapshot: where it left from, where it's headed, what kind of vessel it is (a container ship, say), plus a timeline of milestones and key logistics events, actual departure time, predictive ETA, the details that actually matter for planning.

Open the card further and the full vessel profile shows up: vessel name, IMO and MMSI numbers, the flag it sails under, vessel type, build year, dimensions like length and beam, and where it was last spotted.

### Step 4: Route tab
Here's where the journey gets mapped end-to-end: live events, route changes, dynamic status updates, and forecasts flagging potential delays ahead of time, all tied to the vessel's actual voyage.

Route exceptions show up here too, keeping logistics teams aligned with what the ship is actually doing right now.

### Step 5: Schedule tab
Port call timing and arrival forecasts stay current in the Schedule tab.

Supply chain managers use it to line up pickups and deliveries against the vessel's newest arrival estimate, plus terminal data and projected departure windows, tracked continuously. Fewer missed pickups, less last-minute rescheduling.

### Step 6: Share the tracking link with others
One click generates a shareable tracking link. Send it to teams, partners, or customers, and everyone sees the same real-time picture on that particular vessel. Less manual reporting, smoother communication on shipping operations overall.

## Vessel Tracking questions, answered

### How do I track a ship's position in real time?
Open SeaRates Vessel Tracking and type in the vessel's name, IMO number, or MMSI number. The tool shows where it is right now, along with voyage progress, route details, and an expected arrival date.

### What kind of data does the tool show?
You get the vessel's live position, its schedule and route, movement details between ports, a full timetable, and a predicted arrival, all pulled from AIS maritime technology.

### Can I look up a container ship using just its IMO number?
Yes, that's enough on its own. Punch in the IMO number and you'll get its location and voyage. Searching by vessel name or MMSI number gets you the same result.

### How is an IMO number different from an MMSI number?
The International Maritime Organization assigns each ship an individual IMO identification number. The Maritime Mobile Service Identity number, MMSI, handles communication activities and AIS tracking operations. SeaRates Vessel Tracking lets you search by either one, or by vessel name.

### Does the tool update ETAs as conditions change?
Yes, predictive ETA monitoring is built in. It estimates how long a vessel still needs to reach port and flags schedule changes for the logistics team early.

### Is it possible to share tracking data with customers?
Yes. A shareable vessel page link works for customers and internal teams alike, anyone who needs real-time visibility into a shipment's movement.

### Is there an API for vessel tracking?
Yes. The SeaRates Vessel Tracking API lets trade and shipping companies pull vessel tracking details into their own systems in real time, including voyage and arrival data.
