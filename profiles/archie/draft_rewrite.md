META DESCRIPTION: Explore SeaRates Week 20 2025 updates: Terminal Tracking API docs, pricing plans, Thai Vietair support, and container tracking exceptions.

Week 20 of 2025 brings software revisions and data updates across our multi-modal logistics software platform.

### Schedules and Tracking Subscriptions
Dedicated pricing plans are now live for our Ship Schedules and Air Tracking tools. Users can choose subscription options to fit their operational needs, whether setting up browser tools or integrating via our ship schedules API.

### Terminal Tracking Documentation
Documentation for the terminal tracking API is now published on the Developer Portal. Developers can inspect request schemas and query our database of ocean and land terminals for wider tracking coverage.

### Container Tracking Exceptions and ETAs
We updated the container tracking exceptions tab and refined our predictive ETA logic.

Carrier integration data was updated for thirteen ocean lines:
* Turkon
* Pacific International Lines (PIL)
* Gold Star Line
* Yang Ming
* COSCO
* Evergreen
* Heung-A Shipping
* W.E.C. (West European Container) Lines
* Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha (K Line)
* Sinokor
* Wan Hai
* Emirates Shipping Line
* DHL Global Forwarding

Schedule queries now handle Gold Star Line and Turkon by Points, while Evergreen lookups operate by Port.

### Air Freight Tracking
Thai Vietair joins our air freight tracking network. That brings total supported airlines to 446.

Tracking support was also upgraded for six existing carriers: Air India, FedEx Express, China Southern Airlines, My Indo Airlines, Air Canada, and ASL Airlines Belgium.

### Location Data and Page Redesigns
Our geocoding autocomplete service now includes alternative names for 217 world capitals and the top 100 ports. The tool now handles lookups across 8 major languages.

We also updated the content and visual design for three core product pages: Bulk/Break Bulk, LCL (Less Container Load), and FCL (Full Container Load).
