## Week 49 shipped

November is nearly over. Before it goes, here's everything that changed on SeaRates this week, from the mobile app down to the geocoding database.

The SeaRates Mobile App for Android now has Chat. Requests, shipment tracking, booking questions, all of the back-and-forth with your logistics partners can happen from your pocket, wherever you are.

## Container tracking and air tracking

On the ocean side, we improved how we work with shipping lines, including Turkon, Sea Hawk Lines (SHAL), MSC, Wan Hai, ZIM, and Gold Star Line. If you follow containers on these lines, expect smoother data coming back.

Air freight had its own batch of work. For the Air Tracking API we updated airline support to cover Saudi, Lufthansa Cargo, Swiss International Air Lines, Juneyao Airlines, SolitAir Express, Plus Ultra, Norse Atlantic Airways, Express Air Cargo, Evelop Airlines, Air Belgium, Royal Air Maroc, and Egyptair. Real-time shipment tracking covers these carriers.

## Around the platform

The Geocoding API saw work this week. Country and city data was processed and refreshed in the database, extended imports ran for roughly 250 countries, and search scoring improved. Small things individually, but together they make every lookup feel quicker.

Logistics Map got faster too, across all three tabs: Cargoes, Transport, Warehouses. And over in Virtual Office, the Request System got an update aimed at better request optimization.

Thank you for staying with us. Your requirements keep shifting and we intend to keep up.
