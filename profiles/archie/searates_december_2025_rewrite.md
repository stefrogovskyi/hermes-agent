# December 2025 Release Notes: Smoother Tracking, Sharper Freight Rates

meta_title: December 2025 SeaRates Update: Tracking, Rates, Booking
meta_description: SeaRates December 2025 release notes: container tracking design updates, Android chat inbox, new ship schedule carriers, and freight rate API improvements.

A month of small, stubborn fixes adds up. That was the shape of our December work on the SeaRates digital logistics platform: stability first, then performance, then a long list of practical touches across services. If you skipped last month's release notes, read those first. The context helps.

## Tracking System

The unified design of the all-in-one Tracking System keeps moving toward its final form. Container Tracking, Air Tracking, Rail Tracking, and Road Tracking all live inside it now, one real-time monitoring solution for every mode you ship on.

## Mobile App

Android users, this one is yours. The Chat System is now built into the SeaRates App as a smart Inbox tied to shipment tracking, booking management, and request handling. Logistics communication in your pocket, wherever the day drags you. iOS got this earlier; if that announcement passed you by, the App has been waiting there all along.

## Ship Schedules

Two new carriers joined the schedules: Royal Arctic Line, searchable by both Port and Vessel tabs, and Dongjin Shipping by Vessel. The real-time world map on ship schedule result cards also got a refresh.

## Logistics Map

Design and performance work continues here. Coordinate accuracy was updated across all three tabs: Cargoes, Transport, Warehouses.

## Logistics Explorer

Air shipments now get improved freight rate calculation inside the Logistics Explorer. On the developer side, the freight calculator API gained an integrated Air Cargo API, with updated documentation on the Developer Portal to match. Freight rates at a new level of accuracy, for teams who build their own tools.

## Carrier Directory

Data synchronization improved across the board, which shows up in better tracking results and sharper carrier searches for ship schedules.

## Virtual Office

The busiest section this month. The Transport, Request, and Counterparties tabs were optimized for speed, along with the Inbox in your Profile. In the Booking System, the Payment tab got attention and new customization options landed for design. The Booking System API itself performs better after this round of improvements, and its documentation on the Developer Portal is updated accordingly. Rate Management System changes round it out: the Customize option was refined across several tabs, with general performance and design polish throughout.
