A truck leaves the depot and, for most of the next few hundred kilometers, its position is a guess. SeaRates and LandRates built the Road Tracking tool to replace the guesswork with something closer to a live digital twin of the cargo: one screen where land freight stays visible while it moves, whether you are watching a single truck or a batch of them crossing borders.

## Where to find it and how to start

The tool sits on both SeaRates.com (top menu, or the Tools page) and LandRates.com (top menu). Open it after your booking is confirmed, choose the Road shipment type under All carrier, and enter the shipment number or tracking number your carrier gave you.

Got a pile of shipments? Request multi-tracking and upload a file with the whole list of tracking numbers. They all get monitored together, in one place. That alone removes the old routine of hopping between carrier portals and transport company websites to piece together updates by hand.

## The shipment card

Results arrive as a shipment card with the current state of your road cargo:

- Status updates in real time: Booked / Received / Departed / In transit / Arrived / Out for delivery / Delivered / Exception / Canceled
- Cargo details: number of packages and total weight
- Tracking number and carrier logo
- Origin and destination points
- Predictive and actual ETA, plus other live logistics data

This is where predictive analytics stops being a buzzword and starts being a column on a card. When the estimated arrival shifts, you see it shift, and your team can react to a delay while it is still hours away rather than after the customer calls.

## History and Route tabs

The live map draws truck movements as they happen, so the location you see stays close to the location that is real. Two tabs on the card go deeper.

History holds the full timeline and events log: pickup and delivery confirmations, delay and exception events, proof of delivery where the carrier provides it.

Route covers the journey itself. You get route milestones (pickup, in transit, out for delivery, delivered, exception), any extra information the carrier supplies, and, where available, historical ETA accuracy for that route.

That last item deserves a pause. Historical ETA accuracy is the raw material of route optimization: compare what carriers promised against what they actually delivered, and your next booking decision gets made on data-driven planning rather than whoever had the best sales pitch. It also feeds an on-time delivery performance analysis, so future road shipments can be planned around verified numbers.

One practical touch: copy the link to the tracking card and share it with a customer or partner. They see the same live view you do, and your inbox gets a little quieter.

## Cross-border and multi-stop cargo

Route changes show up on the card, which matters most when a shipment is complicated. Cross-border road transport and multi-stop deliveries are exactly the cases where a missed update early in the journey turns into a late delivery at the end. Keeping early delay detection and the full route in one solution is the point of the tool.

## Last-mile tracking with GPS and telematics

If your business includes urban distribution or time-critical deliveries, Road Tracking can be extended with GPS and telematics integration, ELD and hardware data sources, and driver-based mobile tracking.

What that unlocks in practice is last-mile delivery visibility: continuous truck monitoring, accurate stop detection, proof-of-delivery workflows, and last-mile customer notifications, all feeding into the same tracking view. The final kilometers of a shipment are usually where customers get nervous, and this is the part of the chain the extension is built to cover.

## Your brand on the tracking page

SeaRates offers a branded tracking page too, a white-label web widget you can embed into your own website or customer portals.

Once it is live, shippers see transit times, route details, and live carrier updates on their shipments from your source, under your logo. It turns a tracking chore into a reason for customers to keep visiting your site.

## API: dashboards and custom builds

The Road Tracking API connects the tool to your TMS, ERP, or CRM system. From there you can automate real-time road shipment tracking, pull predictive ETA for trucks into your own workflows, and build custom real-time dashboards or customer portals around the data.

If you would rather build your own monitoring app or an enterprise road tracking solution on top of the SeaRates Road Tracking SaaS, the API documentation is available at the Developer Portal. For a demo or a customized integration, drop a message at it.sales@searates.com.

## A short word on why this matters

Road freight has lagged ocean and air on visibility for years, mostly because trucks change hands between carriers, subcontractors, and border crossings. Tools like this close that gap by normalizing whatever the carrier reports into one card with one ETA. You do not need a data science team to run it; you need a tracking number.

Start with one shipment, watch how the predictive and actual ETA compare on a route you know well, and go from there. The History tab will tell you pretty quickly which carriers deserve your next booking.
