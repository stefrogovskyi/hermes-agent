TITLE: SeaRates April Release: Booking Goes Mobile

META-TITLE: SeaRates April 2026: Mobile Booking, Wider Carrier Reach

META-DESCRIPTION: SeaRates' April release adds mobile booking, 9 new carriers (237 total), an upgraded Distance & Time tool, and new AirRates Flight Schedules.

BODY:

Spring showed up this year with a toolbox instead of flowers, and SeaRates spent the past few weeks putting it to work.

We've spent the month tightening things across the platform, some changes visible to every user, some the kind of back-end fixes only your ops team will notice. If April's news catches you off guard, last month's release notes fill in the background this update builds on.

## Carrier Coverage Widens Again

SeaRates keeps stacking up shipping line partnerships, and this round adds nine: Ocean Silk Line, Cleve & Zonen, Hankyu Hanshin Express, Quanterm Logistics, Shandong Marine Corporation, Shanghai League Shipping, WeFreight, RTW Logistics, and Feeder Logistics. That's the whole list. Combined with everything already on the platform, the Container Tracking API now gives you access to up to 237 shipping lines, leasing companies, and carriers for real-time visibility into container movements.

## Distance & Time, Rebuilt

The Distance & Time tool got an overhaul. Enter your origin, pick a transportation mode, set the destination, and it works out accurate route distance and transit time. The safe routing mode has also been sharpened: you can now estimate and compare trade lanes side by side to find the most efficient option for a given shipment, useful groundwork for anyone weighing freight forwarding software decisions.

## Booking, Now in Your Pocket (and Sharper on the Back End)

The headline feature this month: the Booking System has landed inside the SeaRates Mobile App. Bookings, progress tracking, payments, general control over your logistics operations, all of it now lives on your phone. Grab the app on Android or iOS and manage your shipments from wherever you happen to be, which is really the point of mobile logistics management.

On the desktop and API side, the Booking API picked up new booking options tied to selected schedules, with documentation refreshed on the Developer Portal, a solid step for anyone leaning on booking automation through API integration. The Payment tab was upgraded too, so handling the financial side of a booking is more efficient whether you're working through the Virtual Office or connecting via the API.

## All Shipping Leads, Reworked

A new version of All Shipping Leads is live. It's built to help you find, generate, and attract leads for sea freight services, with improved tools designed to support business growth.

## Geocoding API Gets Heavier

The global ports classification database now covers more than 5,500 river ports and over 12,500 seaports worldwide, a serious jump in scale for anyone relying on port and terminal data. New filters make generating port lists easier, autocomplete logic has been improved with new port types, and the integration with the Freight Index tool is tighter. On top of that, city data covering more than 2,000 locations globally has been expanded.

## LandRates Keeps Building Out

Three additions here. The Logistics Map now includes a Warehouse tab, useful for storage management and for promoting services through the LandRates marketplace. A Freight Index tool has also been introduced for benchmarking and market analysis in land logistics. And Rail Tracking finally has its own dedicated benefits page for web access.

## AirRates Adds Flight Schedules

Flight Schedules is now live on AirRates.com. Browse airline timetables and deadlines across global routes and carriers, then share schedule updates with customers and partners through unique access links, another small but useful piece of the supply chain visibility puzzle. New landing pages went up to support it too: a Pricing page for the Flight Schedules tool and a separate page for the API service.

April kept the pace up across the board on this digital freight platform. If you want the play-by-play as it happens, SeaRates posts updates on LinkedIn, X, Facebook, and Instagram.
