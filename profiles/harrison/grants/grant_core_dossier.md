## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ELEVATOR PITCH
Navo24 is a modular, AI-MCP (Model Context Protocol) native maritime and ocean-freight intelligence infrastructure. Navo24 unifies 233 shipping lines, 125 direct connectors, live AIS satellite vessel feeds, and DCSA-compliant event normalisation into an open, callable data spine. 

By exposing container tracking, sailing schedules, load optimization (CTU Code / IMDG), and freight rate intelligence as callable AI tools, Navo24 eliminates data blackouts across global supply chains, reduces port congestion delays by up to 28%, and cuts maritime carbon emissions by optimizing container stowage and routing efficiency.

## 2. KEY PILLARS & GRANT ALIGNMENT THEMES

### Pillar A: AI, Agentic Workflows & Digital Infrastructure
* **Focus:** Next-generation AI-to-Software integration via Model Context Protocol (MCP).
* **Innovation:** First-party MCP servers for Tracking, Schedules, AirCargo, Loading, and Rates, allowing LLM agents (Claude, GPT, Gemini, enterprise assistants) to autonomously query, diagnose logistics bottlenecks, and execute supply chain actions without custom middleware.
* **Target Grants:** Innovate UK BridgeAI, Horizon Europe AI & Data Infrastructure, Qatar QRDI Product Development Grant.

### Pillar B: Green Maritime, Decarbonization & Net Zero
* **Focus:** Fuel reduction, container stowage optimization, and route efficiency.
* **Innovation:** `LoadingMCP` integrates CTU Code, EN 12195, and IMDG weight/axle distribution models, maximizing container utilization (averaging 91% vs industry 72%), reducing unnecessary container sailings and associated bunker fuel emissions.
* **Target Grants:** EU Horizon Europe Climate & Green Shipping, Innovate UK Clean Maritime, UK Net Zero Innovation.

### Pillar C: Supply Chain Resilience, Global Trade & Customs
* **Focus:** Cross-border visibility, multimodal tracking, sanctions compliance.
* **Innovation:** DCSA-standardized milestone normalization across 233 ocean carriers, automated demurrage/detention prediction, and multi-source failover.
* **Target Grants:** EU FSTP/Cascade Funding (SMART4ALL, DIH World, SecurIT), Innovate UK Smart Grants, Middle East Logistics Hub Grants (QSTP, Hub71).
