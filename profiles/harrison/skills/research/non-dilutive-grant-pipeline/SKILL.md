# Non-Dilutive Grant Scouting & Application Pipeline

A structured framework for scouting, evaluating, and applying for equity-free (non-dilutive) public innovation grants, cascade funding, and regional R&D subsidies (UK, EU, Middle East / Gulf).

## When to Use
- Searching, evaluating, and tracking open grant calls (Innovate UK, Horizon Europe, QRDI, Hub71).
- Assembling modular grant core dossiers (company particulars, TRL, IP, decarbonization, market impact).
- Formulating fast-track cascade applications (FSTP) and multi-million innovation proposals.

## Core Operational Workflow

### 1. Grant Tiering & Velocity Matrix

| Tier | Grant Type & Scale | Typical Timeframe | Key Focus Areas |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Fast-Track / Cascade)** | **£20k – £60k (€30k–€60k)**<br>• EU FSTP (F6S / FundingBox)<br>• Innovate UK BridgeAI single vouchers | 4–8 weeks | 5–10 page lightweight proposals. Rapid proof-of-concept, AI workflow adoption in transport/logistics, open standards. |
| **Tier 2 (Targeted Innovation)** | **£100k – £500k ($500k)**<br>• Innovate UK Smart Grants<br>• Qatar QRDI Product Development Grant (PDG)<br>• UAE NTDP / Hub71 Subsidies | 2–4 months | Commercialization of TRL 6–8 platforms, regional logistics hub pilots (Hamad / Jebel Ali), supply chain resilience. |
| **Tier 3 (Flagship Moonshots)** | **€500k – €2.5M**<br>• EIC Accelerator (Horizon Europe)<br>• Clean Maritime Demonstration | 4–9 months | Groundbreaking R&D, net-zero maritime decarbonization, massive scale. |

### 2. The Modular Grant Core Pack Pattern
To submit high-velocity applications without starting from scratch:
1. **Executive Summary / Value Proposition:** Clear 150-word elevator pitch emphasizing AI-native infrastructure, DCSA standards, and market size.
2. **Company & Legal Particulars:** Official entity details (WEMELOGISTICS LTD, UK Co. No. 14081751, SIC codes 50200/62012, 100% IP ownership, TRL 7–8).
3. **Decarbonization / Green Impact:** Concrete CO2 reduction metrics (e.g. `LoadingMCP` load optimization reducing container sailings).
4. **Work Packages & Deliverables:** Standardized 4-phase milestone structure (Ingestion & Schema → Model Tuning → Pilot Testing → Commercial Rollout).

### 3. Application & Communication Rules
- **Outreach & Inquiries:** Always initiate correspondence from `contact@navo24.com`, signed as General Legal Counsel & Chief Compliance Officer.
- **Grant Tracker Persistence:** Maintain a structured JSON/MD tracker recording `id`, `funder`, `amount`, `deadline`, `tier`, `status`, and `target_product`.
