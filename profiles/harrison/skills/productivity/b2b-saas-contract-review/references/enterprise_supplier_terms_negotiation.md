# Enterprise Supplier Terms vs. SaaS Data Licensing Playbook

## The Core Conflict: Procurement Terms vs. Data API Model
Large enterprise customers (e.g. Lloyd's List Intelligence, S&P, maritime data aggregators) often mandate their standard "Supplier Terms and Conditions". These templates are authored for physical goods procurement or bespoke work-for-hire software development, creating major legal traps for real-time data API providers:

1. **Unlimited / Consequential Liability:** Supplier terms frequently include indemnities for third-party loss of revenue or replacement service acquisition costs.
2. **Fitness for Purpose & Third-Party Carrier Data:** Warranting 100% data timeliness and accuracy on external carrier/port feeds is impossible in global maritime logistics.
3. **Overriding Precedence:** Standard procurement terms state they supersede any provider quotes or online agreements.

## 4-Step Negotiation Strategy

### Step 1: Precedence Demarcation
Never allow the customer's Supplier Terms to hold overall precedence. Maintain the **Critical Order of Precedence** where Part 1 (Commercial Offer & Special Conditions) governs all commercial terms, data licensing, SLA, and liability limits.

### Step 2: Carve-Out Compliance vs. Operational Terms
Accept the customer's legitimate corporate governance requirements as an Annex:
* UK Bribery Act 2010
* Modern Slavery Act 2015
* UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018
* Sanctions & Export Controls (OFAC, UK HMT, EU)
Explicitly exclude procurement remedies, third-party substitute data cost recovery, and work-for-hire IP assignment.

### Step 3: Liability Cap Compromise ("Super-Cap")
Instead of uncapped liability or minimal monthly fee caps:
* Agree to direct damages capped at **the greater of 100% of 12-month fees or £25,000 GBP**.
* Strictly retain mutual exclusion of indirect, punitive, and consequential losses (loss of business/profit).

### Step 4: Express Commercial Data Licensing
Insert unambiguous data redistribution terms:
* **Permitted:** Ingesting, enriching, combining, and displaying data within customer application platforms and customer-facing reports for end subscribers.
* **Prohibited:** Standalone redistribution, sublicensing, or bulk resale of raw API data feeds without value-add product integration.
