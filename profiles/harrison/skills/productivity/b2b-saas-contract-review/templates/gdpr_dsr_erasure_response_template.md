# GDPR Data Subject Request (Right to Erasure & De-escalation) Template

Use when a prospective B2B client or outreach recipient accuses an Account Executive (AE) of data privacy violations, contact theft, or threatens regulatory filings (GDPR / DPA).

## Protocol & De-escalation Workflow

1. **Immediate AE Silence:** The Account Executive must NEVER reply directly from their personal inbox or debate the prospect.
2. **CRM Suppression:** Immediately tag all prospect email addresses and phone numbers in the CRM / outreach engine as `Suppressed / Do Not Contact`.
3. **Official Channel Routing:** Dispatch the formal response exclusively from the official privacy mailbox (`privacy@e.navo24.com` or `privacy@navo24.com`), signed by General Legal Counsel / Data Protection Officer.
4. **Copy Relevant Stakeholders:** CC executive leadership (`stefan@navo24.com`), the originating AE (`<name>@navo24.com`), and `legal@navo24.com`.

---

## Standard DSR Response Template

**From:** Navo24 Privacy & Compliance Office `<privacy@e.navo24.com>`  
**To:** `[Prospect Email]`  
**Cc:** `[Alternate Prospect Email, if any]`, `[AE Email]`, `stefan@navo24.com`, `legal@navo24.com`  
**Reply-To:** `privacy@navo24.com`  
**Subject:** Data Subject Request & Privacy Confirmation // Case Ref: DSR-[YYYY-MMDD]-[INITIALS]
