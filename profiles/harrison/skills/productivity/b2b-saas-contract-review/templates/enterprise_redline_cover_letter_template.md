# Enterprise SaaS Redline & Counter-Proposal Cover Letter Template

Use this template when sending back redlines, Special Conditions, or counter-proposals to enterprise procurement and legal teams (e.g. LLI, EY, S&P, tier-1 customers).

---

```markdown
Subject: Re: [Company Provider] / [Customer Name] — [Agreement Name] & Final Review

Dear [Customer Legal / Procurement / Deal Team],

Thank you for providing the updated draft of the [Agreement Name / Commercial Offer] for the [Product/Platform Name] subscription.

We are very pleased with our ongoing commercial partnership and have reviewed the draft with a focus on finalizing the contract swiftly. We have accepted the vast majority of your proposed points and made only a few targeted adjustments to ensure the agreement accurately reflects a multi-tenant SaaS / API data service.

Please find our comments and the rationale behind the minor adjustments below:

---

### 1. Order of Precedence
* **Adjustment:** We have clarified the priority hierarchy so that the agreed **Part 1 (Special Conditions / Order Form)** and **Part 2 (SaaS Platform Terms)** govern the software service, data licensing, and operational liability, while [Customer]'s Standard Terms for Suppliers apply supplementarily to the extent they do not conflict with Parts 1 & 2.
* **Commercial Rationale:** As [Platform Name] is a standardized, automated data intelligence API platform, operational parameters (such as the agreed liability cap, API licensing boundaries, uptime targets, and third-party data disclaimers) must be governed by the agreed Special Conditions. Incorporating supplier terms designed for general procurement above the SaaS agreement would inadvertently create legal ambiguities regarding platform IP ownership and third-party carrier/source data disclaimers.

### 2. Service Scope & Custom Development / Connectors
* **Adjustment:** We refined the wording around adding new connectors/sources to reflect our commitment to assess requests in good faith and schedule connectors within our active development roadmap at no extra cost, where technically and commercially viable.
* **Commercial Rationale:** While [Provider] is committed to expanding source coverage for [Customer], connectivity depends on external third-party API/EDI availability. This adjustment ensures we can manage integrations responsibly without assuming open-ended custom development liabilities.

### 3. Artificial Intelligence & Data Integrity Warranty
* **Adjustment:** We have confirmed that [Provider] does not deliver synthetic/hallucinated data generated entirely by generative AI, while clarifying that automated algorithmic parsing, normalization, OCR, and data validation routines remain part of the core data ingestion pipeline.
* **Commercial Rationale:** [Platform Name] surfaces actual operational industry data. Clarifying this ensures our algorithmic ETL/processing pipelines remain fully compliant with customer data integrity policies.

### 4. Execution Details & Drafting Clean-Up
We have populated and aligned the operational parameters to finalize the document for execution:
* **Payment Terms:** Set to standard **Net 30 calendar days** from the receipt of a valid VAT invoice.
* **Initial Term:** Fixed at **12 months** with standard annual auto-renewal and 60 days' notice prior to expiry.
* **Service Level Target:** Formally documented at **[99.5%] Platform Availability Target** (excluding scheduled off-peak maintenance).
* **Drafting Adjustments:** Completed placeholders and grammatical alignment across clauses.

---

We have attached the updated clean version along with a redline showing these minor refinements. 

Please let us know if these adjustments are acceptable, and we will be delighted to move directly to e-signature.

Kind regards,

[Authorised Signatory Name]  
[Title, Company Provider]  
*cc: Legal Counsel & Deal Desk*
```
