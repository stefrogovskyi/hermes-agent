# B2B Sales Outreach: 4-Touch Sequence & Signature Rules

## 4-Touch Sequence Pattern for Chinese Freight Forwarders (CN FF)

When reaching out to cold B2B prospects (e.g. Chinese logistics operators, freight forwarders, NVOCCs):

### Touch 1: Short Catchy Hook (Емейл 1)
- **Goal**: Grab attention, establish relevance, and ask a short open question to gauge interest.
- **Tone**: Professional, direct, human.
- **Length**: Very short (3–4 sentences).
- **Structure**:
  1. Greeting (`[Contact Person] 您好！`)
  2. One-line intro (Navo24 UK ocean data platform).
  3. One-line hook question (Are you looking for efficient ocean container tracking and flexible API/MCP data integration?).
  4. Polite closing + HTML Signature.

### Touch 2 & 3: Dialogue Building (Емейлы 2 и 3)
- **Goal**: Engage in consultative dialogue based on their specific trade lanes / carriers.
- **Tone**: Helpful freight desk consultant.
- **Content**: Ask clarifying questions regarding primary shipping lines, trade lanes, operational pain points (D&D demurrage leaks, stale ETAs), or decision-makers.

### Touch 4: Full Value Proposal (Емейл 4)
- **Goal**: Present the full value proposition and callable tool suite.
- **Content**: Detailed breakdown of TrackingMCP, SchedulesMCP, LoadingMCP, FreightRatesMCP, DCSA normalization, observed arrival reliability, and a clear CTA to test or evaluate.
