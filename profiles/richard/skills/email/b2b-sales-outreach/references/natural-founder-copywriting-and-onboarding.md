# Natural Founder-Led B2B Copywriting, Onboarding & Zero-Fabrication Standards (2026)

## 1. Natural Founder-Led Cold Email Standard (40–55 Words)
1. **Never pitch aggressively or use AI rhetorical agitation**: Avoid "Do your operations teams struggle with..." or "When carrier schedules are off...".
2. **Open naturally from first person with SeaRates pedigree**:
   *"We built Navo24 to give forwarders direct tracking across 239 ocean lines, live satellite AIS positions, and predictive ETAs that account for actual port congestion (founded by the original team behind SeaRates)."*
3. **Brevity**: Strictly 40–55 words (3 short paragraphs).
4. **Subject line**: 2–3 words, casual internal lowercase (`{domain} / container tracking`).
5. **Zero-friction CTA**:
   *"You can test a couple of your active shipments on our free tier at https://trackingmcp.com/auth/signup to see how the data looks against your carrier updates. Worth taking a look?"*

## 2. Strict Zero-Fabrication Policy (Anti-Hallucination)
- When a client reports technical benchmark errors (HTTP 502/504), tracking anomalies (vessel rollovers), DCSA mapping bugs, or unlisted endpoints:
- **NEVER construct plausible-sounding technical explanations** or invent backend architecture behavior.
- **STOP and Escalate**: Clearly state that verified facts from the engineering team are needed, and escalate to the internal team.

## 3. Internal Team Escalation Format (Telegram Group)
- Keep messages collegial, concise, and without spammy emojis:
  *"Всем привет еще раз, у меня фидбек по триалу от клиента... [номера контейнеров, суть бага, точный вопрос клиента]"*.

## 4. Commercial Starter Tier (Up to 25 Shipments)
- **Service**: Container Tracking (API connection)
- **Allowance**: 1 up to 25 unique shipments/mo (by Container, B/L, or Booking)
- **API Calls**: 750 calls/month
- **Cost**: USD 50/month or USD 500/year (No separate API license fee).
- **Billing Logic**: Unique shipment counted once per calendar month. Repeated requests consume API calls only. Webhooks included.
- **Onboarding Flow**: User registers at `https://trackingmcp.com/auth/signup` -> sends email -> AE arranges trial access.
